Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from suppliers

You can list any fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) that are suitable for spaza shops, including food and beverages, household products, personal care items, and more. Products must be in good condition with at least 4 weeks remaining before expiry.

You set a minimum acceptable bid for your inventory. Spaza shops then bid against each other, driving the price up. You'll always receive at least your minimum bid, but often much more. Our platform suggests competitive minimum bids based on market data.

You can use your own delivery network or our trusted logistics partners who specialize in last-mile delivery to spaza shops. Our partners offer competitive rates and can collect from your warehouse.

Payment is released to your account within 48 hours of delivery confirmation. We deduct our 7% platform fee before transferring funds to your designated bank account via EFT.

We recommend lots of at least R5,000 in value to make delivery economical, but there's no strict minimum. Smaller lots can be combined with other suppliers' products going to the same area.
